03 / MY APPROACH

A practical way
to catch what matters.

I prefer repeatable checks and straightforward communication. The goal is not just to find an issue—it’s to help the team resolve it.

Talk through my process ↗
  1. 01

    Understand the brief

    Review the references, requirements, formats, and acceptance criteria.

  2. 02

    Inspect the creative

    Check the asset’s structure, appearance, and expected functionality.

  3. 03

    Compare and test

    Use visual, frame-by-frame, and relevant scenario-based checks.

  4. 04

    Document clearly

    Capture what differs, where it happens, and how it can be reproduced.

  5. 05

    Re-test the fix

    Verify the correction and look for related issues before closing out.
